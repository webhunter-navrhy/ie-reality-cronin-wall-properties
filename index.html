Description

Cronin Wall Properties are delighted to present No. 35 Elm Grove, Castlemartyr, to the market. This bright and well-presented property is situated within a mature and established residential development, offering well-proportioned accommodation. The property benefits from a bright and spacious open-plan living and dining area, with large glazed doors overlooking the rear garden. The abundance of natural light, neutral décor and contemporary flooring create a fresh and inviting living space, with excellent connection to the garden.

Elm Grove is located in the village of Castlemartyr, with a range of local amenities close by including shops, cafés, restaurants, schools and recreational facilities. The village is also ideally positioned for access to the N25, providing convenient connections towards Cork City, Midleton and East Cork, while the nearby coastline and surrounding countryside provide a variety of options for leisure and recreation.

No. 35 Elm Grove offers well-presented accommodation in a convenient and established Castlemartyr location.
